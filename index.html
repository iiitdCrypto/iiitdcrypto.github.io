Symmetric Cryptography Lab

We study the design and security analysis of symmetric cryptographic primitives — the structural properties of block and stream ciphers, and the cryptanalytic techniques that break them.

Our work extends into quantum cryptanalysis, where we ask what quantum adversaries genuinely change for symmetric-key security, and into machine learning methods that widen the reach of classical attacks.

What we work on

Research Areas

Classical Cryptanalysis

Differential, linear, algebraic and fault-based attacks on block and stream ciphers, and the security margins they expose.

Quantum Cryptanalysis

Grover- and Simon-based attacks assessed at circuit level, so the cost of a quantum adversary is counted honestly rather than assumed.

Design of Symmetric Ciphers

New block and stream cipher constructions, including primitives built for homomorphic-encryption-friendly and low-multiplicative-depth settings.

Machine Learning for Cryptanalysis

Neural distinguishers and learned attack pipelines, and what they can tell us that hand-built distinguishers cannot.
